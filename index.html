THE IDEAS BEHIND THE WORLD

Inspired by research.
Built for exploration.

This lab connects local rules, collective behavior, and inherited variation. It is a deliberately small artificial-life model, with assumptions you can inspect and experiments you can repeat.

A model you can question

The landscape is an illustration. The simulation models bounded positions, food, energy, predation, inherited traits, and a fading signal field. It does not model terrain physics, learned intelligence, real species, or an unlimited evolutionary process.
